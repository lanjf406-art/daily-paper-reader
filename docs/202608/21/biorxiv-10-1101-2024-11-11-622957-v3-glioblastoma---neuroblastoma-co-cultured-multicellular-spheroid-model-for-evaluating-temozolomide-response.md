## 摘要
胶质母细胞瘤是最致命的一种原发性脑肿瘤，对其治疗耐药性的理解日益趋向于不仅源于肿瘤细胞本身，还源于其与周围神经回路的整合。然而，现有的临床前球体模型均未能在快速、可扩展的3D系统中单独评估神经元细胞对胶质母细胞瘤药物反应的直接影响。我们通过琼脂糖液体覆盖法，将胶质母细胞瘤（U87-MG）与神经元谱系细胞群（SH-SY5Y）结合，开发了一种异型3D共培养球体，以直接研究这一空白。随后，在14天内，我们表征了仅胶质母细胞瘤球体、仅神经母细胞瘤球体及其共培养球体的生长、形态、缺氧情况以及替莫唑胺（TMZ）反应。共培养球体呈现出类似胶质母细胞瘤的致密、耐化学治疗结构，然而，与该结构单独预测的结果相反，它在所测试的每个TMZ浓度下均表现出可测量的化学敏感性，包括10μM——这一剂量下两种单一培养物均无任何反应。这表明，神经元谱系组分主动增强了胶质母细胞瘤对化疗的敏感性，且该作用独立于球体结构。因此，仅基于胶质母细胞瘤模型获得的药物反应数据可能无法反映肿瘤在其天然神经元环境中的真实行为，这凸显了在胶质母细胞瘤临床前药物筛选中纳入神经元平台的必要性。

## Abstract
Glioblastoma is the most lethal primary brain tumor, and its resistance to therapy is increasingly understood to arise not from tumor cells alone but also from their integration into the surrounding neural circuitry. However, no existing preclinical spheroid model isolates the direct effect of neuronal cells on glioblastoma's drug response within a rapid, scalable 3D system. We developed a heterotypic 3D co-culture spheroid combining glioblastoma (U87-MG) with a neuronal lineage population (SH-SY5Y) via the agarose liquid overlay method to directly understand this gap. Subsequently, growth, morphology, hypoxia, and temozolomide (TMZ) response over 14 days against glioblastoma only and neuroblastoma only spheroids were characterized. The co-culture adopted a glioblastoma like compact, chemoresistant architecture yet, contrary to what this architecture alone would predict, it showed measurable chemosensitivity at every TMZ concentration tested, including 10M, a dose at which neither monoculture responded at all. This reveals that the neuronal lineage component actively sensitizes glioblastoma to chemotherapy independent of spheroid architecture. Drug-response data from glioblastoma only models may therefore not reflect true tumor behavior within its native neuronal environment, underscoring the need for neuron inclusive platforms in preclinical glioblastoma drug screening.
