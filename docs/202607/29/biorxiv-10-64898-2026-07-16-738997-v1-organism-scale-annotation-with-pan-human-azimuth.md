## 摘要
单细胞图谱现已覆盖多种人体组织，但研究中不一致的注释限制了其作为统一参考的实用性。我们提出了Pan-human Azimuth，这是一个监督神经网络，可将来自不同组织和数据集的细胞映射到单一的分层生物体尺度类型体系上。该模型通过NIH HuBMAP开发，基于一个独特策划的语料库进行训练，旨在最大化组织和技术的多样性，同时强制执行统一、可解释的注释和严格的质量控制。使用单一的生物体范围参考使我们能够映射Tabula Sapiens和scBaseCamp数据库中的数千万个细胞，进行数千个样本的跨组织比较，并识别成纤维细胞状态中显著的组织特化。Pan-human Azimuth自然地扩展到注释空间转录组数据，恢复经典肾皮质结构，并区分与专家病理学一致的肾小球状态。我们发布了Pan-human Azimuth及其云、R和Python接口，以促进标准化的生物体尺度单细胞分析。

## Abstract
Single-cell atlases now span many human tissues, but inconsistent annotations across studies limit their utility as a unified reference. We introduce Pan-human Azimuth, a supervised neural network that maps human cells from diverse tissues and datasets onto a single hierarchical organism-scale typology. Developed through NIH HuBMAP, the model is trained on a uniquely curated corpus designed to maximize diversity across tissues and technologies while enforcing uniform, interpretable annotations and stringent quality control. The use of a single organism-wide reference enables us to map tens of millions of cells in the Tabula Sapiens and scBaseCamp repositories, perform cross-tissue comparisons across thousands of samples, and identify striking tissue specialization among fibroblast states. Pan-human Azimuth naturally extends to annotating spatial transcriptomic data, recovering canonical kidney cortical structures and distinguishing glomerular states consistent with expert pathology. We release Pan-human Azimuth alongside cloud, R, and Python interfaces to facilitate standardized organism-wide single-cell analysis.
